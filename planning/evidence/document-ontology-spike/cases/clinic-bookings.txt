# Small-Animal Clinic: Appointment and Treatment Handbook

Internal handbook, edition 2. Synthetic text prepared for the spike; any resemblance to a real clinic is accidental.

## Overview

The front desk books appointments for pet owners, and the veterinarians examine the animals. This handbook says what is recorded and who decides what.

## Records

An owner is a person responsible for one or more animals. A Treatment Plan (TP) is the list of procedures a veterinarian proposes after an examination.
Each animal belongs to one owner.
Each appointment concerns exactly one animal.
A treatment plan includes the procedures, the estimated cost and the consent form.
An owner has several invoices.
A vaccination is a kind of procedure.

## Statuses

The status of an appointment is one of: requested, confirmed, attended, missed or cancelled.
A treatment plan can be drafted, proposed, accepted or declined.
An invoice moves from issued to paid when the payment is received.

## Rules

The receptionist confirms the appointment by phone. The veterinarian signs the consent form with the owner.
A treatment plan must be approved by the senior veterinarian when its estimated cost exceeds 800 EUR.
Only the practice manager may cancel an invoice.
A controlled drug must never be dispensed without a prescription.
Anaesthesia requires a fasting period of 12 h.

Records are kept according to ISO 27001 and the local veterinary code.

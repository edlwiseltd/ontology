# Autology — expert review of an architect proposal

*17 November 2026. For two independent domain reviewers. Status: **prepared; no reviewer has been engaged; no
score exists.** Part of the proposed evaluation protocol, version 2. A machine-readable copy of this form, with
the elements of one proposal filled in and every judgement empty, is written by
`autology.architect_eval_v2.review_form(case, proposal, reviewer)`.*

## Who reviews

* Someone who has done, or supervised, the work the brief describes.
* Not a member of the project, and not the person who wrote the case.
* Each reviewer works alone; forms are compared afterwards (`agreement`), only on what both judged.

## What the reviewer receives

The brief, the proposal as Studio shows it (elements with their origin and reasons, the questions asked, the
assumptions), and this form. Not the case's expectations, not the automated scores, not the other reviewer's form.

## Scale

1: wrong or unusable · 2: mostly wrong · 3: usable with significant changes · 4: right with small changes ·
5: right as it stands. Leave a field **empty** when it cannot be judged: an empty field is never counted.

## How the forms are used

Scores are reported per reviewer and per criterion, with the share of elements on which the two reviewers are
within one point of each other. Nothing is averaged over a field one of them left empty, and no score is
produced before two forms exist.
